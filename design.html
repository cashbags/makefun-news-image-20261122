The Gulf · Lead

Iran readies a tanker fleet to break the blockade

Tehran is massing 14 or more tankers at Kharg Island, most with their trackers switched off, as reports say a convoy is being readied to test the United States naval blockade. Washington weighs new strikes after a week in which Iran hit American bases in Kuwait and Bahrain and tankers burned in the Strait of Hormuz. Oil through the strait has halved.

The strait carries a fifth of the world’s traded oil. Flows through it fell to 8.5m barrels a day — half the pre-war level. Brent ended the week at $90.83.
Climate · Antalya

Climate talks spill into Sunday with no deal

Nearly 200 countries are deadlocked at COP31 in Antalya over whether the pact should call for a move away from fossil fuels. The Friday deadline passed with no agreement; the latest draft never mentions them at all.

Markets

Wall Street slides into correction

The S&P 500 ended its worst week since 2020 in correction, down 3.7%, as fears over AI spending hit tech. The Nasdaq fell 5.3%; Nvidia fell 13.8% on an SEC accounting probe report.

Mexico · Sinaloa

“El Chapo Isidro” seized — Sinaloa erupts

Mexican forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, an FBI top-ten fugitive, in Guasave on Friday night; two lieutenants were held with him. Gun battles and roadblocks followed across four towns. Mexico confirmed the arrest on Saturday.

United States

FBI probes hack of its crime database

The FBI is investigating an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center, the database police across America use for warrants, fugitives and criminal histories. It is the second FBI intrusion in months; the scope is unclear.

Gaza

Four more killed as the truce frays

An Israeli drone hit a van carrying food to displaced families in Gaza City, killing three; a man was shot dead in Jabalia. 1,671 people have been killed since the October truce, Gaza’s Health Ministry said on Saturday.

Baltic · Gulf of Finland

Drones hit a Russian ship; Finland on alert

A Russian merchant vessel caught fire after it was likely hit by drones in the Gulf of Finland and drifted toward Finnish waters. PM Orpo warned “the war is spreading to our immediate neighbourhood.”

Ukraine · Kyiv

Zelensky: bridge strikes a “fair response”

President Zelensky defended Ukraine’s strikes on the Crimean Bridge as retaliation for 109 Russian attacks on Ukrainian bridges, as Britain’s Wes Streeting visited Kyiv. Russia’s 400-drone barrage on Friday killed eight.

Denmark · Copenhagen

Russia ‘plotted to kill a rapper’, Denmark says

Danish intelligence says Russia’s FSB planned to kill US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner at a September concert. Two men are charged; the Russian defence attaché was expelled. Von der Leyen condemned it on Saturday.
